## What a Balwyn pool barrier inspection covers

An inspection assesses barrier height (minimum 1200mm across every section, including where a house wall or garage wall forms part of the boundary), gate self-closing and self-latching function, gaps under the fence, and the non-climbable zone around the outside face. In Balwyn, where multiple fencing materials and boundary types often combine on a single property, we pay particular attention to the junctions between different barrier sections — where glass fencing meets a house wall, for instance, or where a side boundary transitions to a rear fence — since these transition points are where gaps and inconsistent heights most often slip through.

## Common reasons Balwyn barriers fail first inspection

The most common issue in Balwyn is inconsistency at junction points — where a glass panel meets a masonry wall, or where a house wall forming part of the barrier has a window, door or other opening that doesn't meet the non-climbable requirements a standard fence section would need to satisfy. Ornamental garden design, another Balwyn hallmark, can also encroach on the non-climbable zone when garden beds are built up close to premium fencing for visual effect. Around one in three inspections fail first time across Melbourne, and in Balwyn, complex multi-material barriers are the leading contributor to that figure.
