## What a Caulfield pool barrier inspection covers

Every inspection assesses fence and gate height (minimum 1200mm), gaps under the barrier, the non-climbable zone around its perimeter, and whether gates self-close and self-latch correctly. In Caulfield, where original 1970s and 1980s pool installations are common, we frequently find barriers built to standards that have since been superseded — chain-link or low brick dwarf walls that were compliant decades ago but fall well short of the 1200mm minimum height now required, or gates fitted with simple latches rather than the self-closing, self-latching hardware the current standard requires.

## Common reasons Caulfield barriers fail first inspection

The dominant issue in Caulfield is barriers that were compliant under an older standard but haven't been updated since. Low brick or besser-block dwarf walls, decorative timber fencing with wide picket gaps, and older spring-hinge gates without a proper latch are all common finds. We also see mature established gardens — a hallmark of Caulfield's older residential streets — with hedging or trellis that has grown into the non-climbable zone over many years without anyone noticing. Nationally and locally, around one in three barriers fail their first inspection, and Caulfield's older housing stock puts it at the higher end of that range.
