## What a Point Cook pool barrier inspection covers

An inspection checks fence and gate height (minimum 1200mm), gate self-closing and self-latching function, gaps under the barrier, and the non-climbable zone around its outer face. In Point Cook, where many pools were part of a house-and-land package, we specifically check whether the fencing installed by the builder actually meets the current barrier standard, since display-home-style fencing on a project-built property isn't automatically compliant just because it looks neat and matches the rest of the home's aesthetic.

## Registering your pool with Wyndham City Council

Every pool and spa in Point Cook needs to appear on the Wyndham Pool and Spa Register. Because Wyndham City Council has actively cross-checked aerial imagery against its register in recent years, a number of Point Cook owners have received letters prompting them to register a pool that was otherwise flying under the radar. If that's you, it's worth acting on it sooner rather than later. Once registered and certified, your Form 23 is valid for four years, and we track that expiry so a reminder reaches you ahead of your next required inspection.

## Common reasons Point Cook barriers fail first inspection

The most common issue in Point Cook is builder-supplied fencing that looks compliant but falls short on a specific technical point — often gate self-latching hardware that hasn't been correctly adjusted, or a fence height that's marginally under 1200mm once ground level and pavers are taken into account. We also see garden edging and small retaining walls installed as part of first-time landscaping positioned within the non-climbable zone. Around one in three Melbourne barriers fail their first inspection, and in Point Cook, builder-grade fencing that hasn't been specifically checked against the barrier standard is consistently the leading cause.

## Booking your inspection in Point Cook

The fee is $240 flat, and if issues are found, your first re-inspection once they're corrected is included free, provided it's booked within 60 days. Further re-inspections after that are $90 each. A BPC-registered inspector completes the visit, and a Form 23 Certificate of Barrier Compliance is issued on the day for a pass. You then have 30 days to lodge the certificate with Wyndham City Council, and we send reminders for that deadline and your four-year renewal. Point Cook bookings are typically scheduled within 48 hours.
