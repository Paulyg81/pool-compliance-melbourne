## What a Werribee pool barrier inspection covers

An inspection checks fence and gate height (minimum 1200mm), gate self-closing and self-latching function, gaps under the barrier, and the non-climbable zone around its outer face. In Werribee, we pay close attention to properties that have been partially renovated or subdivided, since it's common to find a barrier where one section was upgraded as part of recent work while an older, adjoining section was left untouched — meaning the fence line as a whole doesn't consistently meet the current standard even though part of it clearly does.
